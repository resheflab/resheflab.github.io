People

Yakir Reshef

Yakir completed a BA in mathematics at Harvard College followed by a Fulbright in probability theory at the Weizmann Institute of Science; a PhD in computer science at Harvard with Ryan Adams, Michael Mitzenmacher, Alkes Price, and Pardis Sabeti; and an MD from Harvard Medical School. He then completed residency in internal medicine and fellowship training in rheumatology at Brigham and Women's Hospital, where he worked with Soumya Raychaudhuri. Yakir likes playing the violin, hiking, reading fiction, and trying to explain complicated things to his kids.
